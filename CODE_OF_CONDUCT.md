# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Windows Observer Agent aims to be a respectful place for people learning or building with Python and AI agents. Experience level, background, identity, and a different technical opinion must not be reasons to exclude someone.

The project currently has one maintainer, tsluyy26. The repository is public; a dedicated conduct-reporting route has not been established.

## Expected Behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence instead of attacking people.
- Ask questions kindly and explain unfamiliar concepts to beginners.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept corrections.
- Respect privacy, authorization boundaries, and other people's work.
- Disclose relevant limitations in tests or AI-assisted contributions.
- Allow others to disagree without pressure or retaliation.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats, targeted malicious attacks, sexual harassment, doxxing, and persistent disruption are not acceptable. Do not share private messages, desktop captures, or identifying information without permission. Do not retaliate against someone raising a good-faith concern.

Technical criticism is welcome; intimidation and personal attacks are not. Using an AI tool does not excuse harmful content or remove the contributor's responsibility.

## Scope

These expectations apply to project-controlled discussions, reviews, future issue/PR spaces, and conduct while representing the project. They do not create a moderation team or imply control over every unrelated platform.

## Reporting Guidelines

**There is currently no dedicated private conduct-reporting channel.** Do not invent an email address or send evidence to an unverified destination. Do not publish private evidence or identifying details in an open thread.

If a suitable private route has already been agreed with the maintainer, use only that route and share the minimum necessary information. Otherwise, ask for a private route without exposing incident details, or wait until one is announced. Security vulnerability details follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Before publicly accepting contributions, the maintainer must publish an appropriate private reporting method and a way to raise a concern involving the maintainer. An independent escalation arrangement is not currently available.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for applying this code fairly and protecting participants' privacy as far as practical. Depending on context, responses may include requesting a correction, removing harmful content, pausing a discussion, or restricting participation.

Actions should be proportionate and consider available evidence. Where safe and possible, the maintainer should explain a decision and allow relevant corrections. No investigation deadline, confidentiality guarantee, appeal panel, or multi-person moderation team is promised.

This is a project-specific conduct policy, not a copied corporate enforcement system.
